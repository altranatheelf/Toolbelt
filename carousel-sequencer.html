Carousel Sequencer

Open a shoot's Lightroom exports

  1. Open the export folder. In Chrome the page remembers it and can reopen it next time.
  2. Every frame gets measured in the background against the set baseline: the median of the strip, or of the whole import until you place something.
  3. Drag frames into the 20-slot strip. Pool order is capture time. The tool never arranges, cuts or captions anything.
  4. Tag each frame: scale W wide, C close, F face, T texture; you in frame 0 no, 1 face hidden, 2 face shown.
  5. The sequence saves as carousel-sequence.json next to the photos.

Nothing leaves this machine. Measuring runs on downscaled copies in a background worker.
