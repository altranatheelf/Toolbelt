How it works, and the Mac engine
    
Each condition is an ffmpeg filter ported to JavaScript (lowpass 800 Hz for wall, a telephone band with a compressor for radio, tape and helium speed changes, ffmpeg's own seeded pink noise for rain, the side channel for sideways, backwards, a 6-bit crusher). Whisper runs through transformers.js on the GPU when the browser offers it, otherwise on the CPU, decoding greedily at temperature 0. The Dissent table aligns words across ears by time and marks hearings that differ from the real line when you give one. The sheet, the sidecar with every parameter, and the ear files are saved together as one .zip.

    
The same tool runs as a Python command with faster-whisper on a Mac (modules/mishear): beam search, the medium model, and the sheet written straight to a folder. The two share the conditions and the sheet format.
